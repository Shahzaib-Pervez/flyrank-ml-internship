Curate Your Images: Visual Discernment & Asset Strategy

👤 Author: Shahzaib Pervez 🔗 Portfolio Context: Machine Learning & Search Intelligence 📅 Date: August 2026
Core Philosophy & Principle

AI permits infinite image generation in seconds, making visual judgment and curation far more critical than raw generation. The goal of this portfolio visual strategy is not to flood the page with synthetic artwork, but to use real screenshots for proof of work, a real photograph for human identity, and strictly cohesive AI generation for non-subject connective tissue (hero backgrounds and icon sets).

2. Rationale: Where Real Captures Beat AI

A. Real Screenshots for Technical Work (Proof of Work)

Decision: Used real captures (`proof_flyrank_roc_pipeline.png` and `proof_research_paper_ui.png`) rather than AI-generated interface mockups.

Rationale: AI-generated dashboards look synthetic and inevitably hallucinate non-existent metric labels or broken code typography. In technical portfolio reviews, hiring managers seek verifiable proof. Real, crisp screenshots of actual DuckDB queries, scikit-learn confusion matrices, and web UI layouts establish instant credibility that synthetic images destroy.

B. Real Photography for Personal Profile

Decision: Used a real high-resolution photograph (`shahzaib_profile_headshot.jpg`).

Rationale: An AI-generated avatar creates an immediate "uncanny valley" response and signals dishonesty regarding identity. Personal trust requires an authentic human photograph.

C. AI Generation for Connective Tissue & Hero Texture

Decision: AI generation was used exclusively for the hero ambient mesh (`hero_dark_network_mesh.png`) and custom minimal icons.

Rationale: Abstract background textures require exact color harmony (HSL dark slate `#0f172a` with cyan/indigo accent mesh) to blend seamlessly with CSS glassmorphism cards without distracting from text readability.

3. Ruthless Curation & Rejection Analysis (The Discernment Test)

Discernment is defined by what you choose not to include. Below is the detailed analysis of AI-generated candidates that were rejected during prompt iteration:

❌ Rejected Candidate #1: "Holographic Cyberpunk Brain Dashboard" (Prompt Iteration 2)

Prompt: "Futuristic glowing brain made of neon blue data circuits floating over dark cybernetic code background, 3D render."

Why Rejected: This image suffered from extreme aesthetic cliché and high visual noise. The neon pink and cyan glow clashed harshly with the portfolio's clean, minimalist dark-mode design system. Furthermore, floating sci-fi brain imagery communicates amateurish tropes rather than serious data engineering capability.

❌ Rejected Candidate #2: "Hyper-realistic 3D Glass Orbs with Code" (Prompt Iteration 5)

Prompt: "Minimalist glass sphere reflecting python code lines, realistic lighting, dramatic depth of field."

Why Rejected: While visually striking in isolation, the lighting direction and heavy drop shadows created an inconsistent "pile" when placed alongside the portfolio cards. The strong specular highlights drew the user's focus away from project headings and broke visual hierarchy.

✅ The Keeper: "Subdued Dark Vector Network Mesh" (Prompt Iteration 8)

Prompt: "Clean abstract dark slate background, subtle deep blue and cyan network nodes connected by ultra-fine faint gradient lines, flat modern vector style, no noise, 16:9 ratio."

Why Kept: It maintains perfect visual harmony with the site CSS tokens (`#0f172a` slate background, `#06b6d4` cyan accents). The low-contrast gradient line weights sit softly behind hero typography without competing for visual attention, forming a cohesive set rather than a chaotic pile.
